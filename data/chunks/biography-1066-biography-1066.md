Indian South Africans Timeline: 1990-1999
1990
23 July, Billy Nair was detained following police allegations of an uMkhonto weSizwe (MK) plot, code-named Operation Vula, to seize power if negotiations failed.  He suffered a heart attack in detention and underwent a heart by-pass operation.  While recuperating, Nair was charged with nine others but granted indemnity from prosecution. When the South African Communist Party (SACP) was relaunched as a legal body on 29 July 1990, Nair was elected to the Central Committee. He was also a member of the interim leadership group of the African National Congress (ANC) in 1990 and was elected to the National Assembly of the South African Parliament in 1994, being 39th on the ANC list of 400 nominees.
1995
1 June, MD Naidoo passes away.
1996
January, George Ponnen passes away in Durban.
1997
April, Karrim Essack of the Non European Unity Movement (NEUM) dies in Dar es Salaam.
1999
1 January, Natoo Babenia passed away.  At the time of his death he was working as a volunteer at the Gandhi Library in Durban.
