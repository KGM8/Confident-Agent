# article-76

He taught art at the Natal Technical College during the 1970s. He exhibited on major group exhibitions of the period and was invited to hold a solo exhibition at the DAG in 1974.
He is a film maker who lives in Cape Town. He is represented in the DAG collection and in private collections in SA.
