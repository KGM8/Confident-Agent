# biography-1381

Born: Caracas, Venezuela, 1972. Resides in Cape Town. Has studied at the Istituto Superiore di Fotografia e Comunicazione Integrata (Italy), University of Miami, and Savannah College of Art and Design, Georgia, USA. She has a BFA (Photography) and Diplomas in Photojournalism and Visual Ethnography. Awards: 2006 Doral Country Club Art Festival Photography Award, Miami, USA.
