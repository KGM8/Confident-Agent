# article-187

Chief Dalindyebo, of the Tembus, was born in 1865. At the age of 14 years he was admitted into St. John's, where he remained until his recalled him to study the aspirations, habits and customs of his people. In 1884 when Dalindyebo was 19 years of age his father died.
