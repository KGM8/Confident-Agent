# article-833

Caiphas Nxumalo graduated from Rorke's Drift in 1971 and during the 1970s was a free-lance artist in Pomeroy. He uses indigenous woods for his sculpture and also produces graphic work.
Exhibitions:
1968: DAM (Art and Craft from Rorke's Drift).
1970: DAM (Sculpture and Ceramics from Rorke's Drift).
1972: SANG(KwaZulu Art of Rorke's Drift).
1979: Marico Moon, Johannesburg (group).
1987: TAG (Rorke's Drift Fine Art School in Retrospect).
UZ: (African Perspectives Symbolism and Religion).
Collections:
KC;UFH.
