# article-9474

Ramafokisi Matthews Mofokeng was banned in July 1966 for two years under the Suppression of Communism Act.

- Order
- Act No. 44 of 1950 Sec. 9 (1)<br><em>Issued Period(s)</em> [28 April 1967 to 31/5/1971]
