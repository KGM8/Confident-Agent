# article-9478

Nobuhle Hillary Mohapi, the widow of Mapetla Mohapi who died in detention, was banned for five years, in 1979, under the Internal Security Act.
Banning order
- Order
- Act No. 44 of 1950 Sec. 9 (1)<br><em>Issued Period(s)</em> [30 June 1979 to 31/12/1983]
