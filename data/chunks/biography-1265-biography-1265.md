# biography-1265

Kutlwano Moagi is a Photographer and an aspiring Jazz Upright Bassist. Born in soweto, 14 April 1983. Completed a photography learnership at the MArket Photo Workshop in 2005 and currently working and living in Joburg. HAs been part of numerous group exhibitions locally and internationally. He is very much interested in the city of joburg and exploring different modes and mediums of working within the city.
