# biography-2497

16 Days of Activism is an international awareness-raising campaign for No Violence Against Women and Children. It takes place every year from 25 November (International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women) to 10 December (International Human Rights Day). The period includes Universal Children&rsquo;s Day and&nbsp;World AIDS Day.
	
The campaign began in 1998 in South Africa as one of the intervention strategies towards creating a society free of violence. The campaign continues to raise&nbsp; awareness amongst South Africans about the negative impact of violence against women and children (VAW&amp;C) on all members of the community.
