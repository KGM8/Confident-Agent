# biography-847

Divested of names and birthright Autshumato and Krotoa were ferried oor die see, oor die see, in die eiland stemertjie&nbsp; every time they incurred the wrath of their insatiable masters. As island-prison you were too small to hold Autshumato (considered to be the devil himself &amp; called Harry die Strandloper) whose nostrils retained the odour of the wild.&nbsp;The irresistible allure of the ancestral spirit-land compelled him to snatch a boat, brave the froth and blinding spray&nbsp; of vessel-snapping swells and with fellow Khoi-comrade row into&nbsp; Blaauwbergstrand, unspotted, dazed, but in one piece! Swift as an air-slicing pestle, he was not re-captured but his land had already slipped into Dutch hands.&nbsp;Robbed of her Khoikhoi identity at eight&nbsp; Krotoa,&nbsp;pygmalioned&nbsp;into a Dutch girl, was baptised and re-named Eva. Subtly programmed to manipulate and betray her people and to serve the Dutch East India Company Krotoa became the most spirited traitor-diplomat of her time. But when the Dutch had eventually grabbed all her political use-value declined. A sudden turn of the wheel of fortune made her service at the Castle obsolete, put her master-mistress foster-parents Jan &amp; Maria Van Riebeeck on a ship to Holland and lured&nbsp; her husband, the respected&nbsp;Posthouder, into a premature death.&nbsp;Thus shaken awake she found no hooks to hold her at the castle, but to what alternative place was she to trek lugging her bewildered self and her young?&nbsp;Estranged from Autshumato and her fast-disappearing people, there remained no Khoikhoi kingdom she could return to. Caught as she was between two cultures, tottering on the rim of the Dutch world, the Khoikhoi world, the Khoi-Dutch world, she threw herself into a cask &amp; hit the bottle hard as she struggled to drown the niggling imagoes of her Khoi-princess past.&nbsp;Khoi princess Krotoa, wrapt in the glory of her favourite skins, chattering away in musical clicks, limbs oiled, vital and glowing, would only be seen as a smelly inboorling, a Hotnot meid wholly and innately dangerous― Never could she dream of reclaiming her birthright, she had irretrievably lost her natural identity―The Company buried baptised Krotoa away from her kindred spirit-soil. Thus ‘earth to earth, ashes to ashes, dust to dust, in sure and certain hope of the resurrection to eternal life...’ Eva, propped into a Dutch coffin, was planted in a remote corner of theCastle-fortress where she had served,had loved &amp; married, was widowed,lost her children, lost her people,her head, heart &amp; carved-up soul― Deela KhanExtract from:&nbsp;Engaging the Shades of Robben Island,&nbsp; (Realities, 2002. pp.22-23)
