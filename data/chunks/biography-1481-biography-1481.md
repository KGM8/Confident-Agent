# biography-1481

I am a self-taught, fine art photographer based in Cape Town. My work is primarily a form of social commentary. I am best known for a series of expressionist, hand-painted photographs depicting marginalized lifestyles in South Africa, which were exhibited at galleries in Germany, the Netherlands and at the &lsquo;Photo LA 2004&rsquo; in Los Angeles, USA. 
	More recently my work has been exhibited by several galleries in South Africa including the Irma Stern Museum the Bell-Roberts Gallery, Focus Contemporary and Kim Sacks Gallery.
	I have recently won an award at the Spier Contemporary 2010 for a self-initiated photographic series documenting informal recyclers in Newtown, Johannesburg.
