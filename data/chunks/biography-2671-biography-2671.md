# biography-2671

South African History Online hosts “virtual internships” with Bridgewater State University students enrolled in a “History of South Africa” course with Dr. Meghan Healy-Clancy, Assistant Professor of History and African Studies Program Coordinator.
The project was inspired by an initial McGill University-SAHO partnership.
The partnership produces essays for SAHO’s expanding history features and biographies. Introducing American students to SAHO and new scholarly efforts in South Africa reveals the significance of historical writing and the promotion of histories that reflect the diversity of experiences and peoples of South Africa.
