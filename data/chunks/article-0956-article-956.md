# article-956

Johannes Mashego Segogela was born in 1936, in Sekhukhuneland, Transvaal. Segogela trained as a boiler-maker. He had no formal art training. For many years he has carved wood sculptures although it is only since 1980 that he has practised as a full-time artist.
