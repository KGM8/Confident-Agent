# article-4160

Robert Cedric Wilcox - a member of the NEUM/Unity Movement (Non-European Unity Movement), APDUSA (the African People’s Democratic Union of Southern Africa), political prisoner, incarcerated for terrorism on Robben Island for six years. After his release from Robben Island, Wilcox continued to build APDUSA. He was also actively involved in the South African Council on Sport (SACOS) in developing the non-racial sports movement in South Africa.
