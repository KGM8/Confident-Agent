# article-291

Gabashane is a graphic artist and ceramic sculptor. He attended the ELC Art and Craft Centre, Rorke’s Drift during the 1960s.
He was an instructor at the Mofolo Art Centre, Soweto.
He has exhibited on group exhibitions in SA and is represented in public and private collections in SA.
