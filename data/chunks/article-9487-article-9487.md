# article-9487

Restricted to Johannesburg. Madibeng Chris Mokoditoa was an Articled clerk, studying to become an attorney. He was the secretary of the University of Christian Movement at the time of his first ban in 1973. In 1975, Mokoditoa was sentenced to one year imprisonment suspended for three years for failing to report to the police.
He was detained from August to December 1976 under the Internal Security Act .
