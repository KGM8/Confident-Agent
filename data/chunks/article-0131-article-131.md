# article-131

She trained at the Evangelical Lutheran Church Art and Craft Centre (ELC), Rorke’s Drift, mainly under Ulla Gowenius and also received some training there under Allina Ndebele.
Very little is known about her.
She exhibited on exhibitions arranged by the ELC Art and Craft Centre in major South African art museums. She is represented in public and private art collections in South Africa and abroad.
