# article-742

Mshololo was born at Umzinyathi, Zululand. As a young boy he carved utilitarian objects such as wooden spoons and meat platters. One of his special interests is carving violins and sculptures of angels.
He has participated in group exhibitions and is represented in public and private galleries in South Africa.
