# biography-981

Detaining and imprisoning those who went against the Apartheid state was one of the many weapons that the government used to quell resistance. As detention was a legal weapon that could see an individual, a group or even an entire community silenced it became more commonly used. As a result deaths in detention particularly during interrogation became more common. From Solwandle Looksmart Ngudle on 3 September 1963 to Alfred Mabake Makaleng on 26 August 1988 there were 67 acknowledged deaths.
