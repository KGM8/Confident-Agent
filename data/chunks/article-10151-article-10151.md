# article-10151

Marelane “Bangilizwe” Joyi, from Thembuland, Umtata District, [Eastern Province, now Eastern Cape] was a sub-chief of 700 homesteads near Umtata.
How we know this
- 01• Contribution by Professor S. Badat on Banishment, Rhodes University, 2012. From the book, Forgotten People - Political Banishment under Apartheid by Professor S. Badat
